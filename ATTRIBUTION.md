# Attribution

Every external asset used by Roofline is listed here with its source and
license. Nothing enters the repository until it has a row in this file.

## Policy

**Allowed:** CC0 / public domain, MIT, BSD, Apache-2.0, and similarly
permissive licenses. CC-BY is acceptable when attributed here.

**Not allowed:** anything ripped from a commercial game; anything with a
non-commercial, no-derivatives, or unclear license; any asset from the
commercial titles this project takes genre inspiration from.

Preference order: **original procedural generation → CC0 → permissive.**
A mediocre original asset beats a borrowed one.
